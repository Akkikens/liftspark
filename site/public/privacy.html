Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Liftspark is a workout, heart-rate and calorie tracker. It has no account and no Liftspark cloud.

What stays on your phone

Everything you log (workouts, plans, body weight, food, settings, and heart-rate traces from a Bluetooth sensor) is stored on your iPhone. It leaves the phone only when you export a backup, share a workout, or turn on iCloud sync (your own private iCloud database, which the developer cannot read).

Apple Health

With your permission Liftspark reads heart rate, workouts, active energy, steps, weight, resting heart rate, HRV and sleep, and writes workouts, weigh-ins and food energy. Health data:

  • is used only to show your training, recovery and calorie numbers in the app;
  • is never sent to the developer, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold;
  • is not stored in Liftspark's backups or exports (only summaries such as a workout's average heart rate are kept on the workout);
  • is never copied to iCloud sync: weigh-ins and sessions imported from Health, and the heart-rate and energy figures Health gives a workout, stay on the phone.

Services you choose to use

  • Food search and barcodes go to Open Food Facts (and, for generic foods, USDA FoodData Central). Only the search text or barcode is sent.
  • Strava, WHOOP and Oura are contacted only after you sign in to them. The sign-in passes through Liftspark's small backend, which exchanges the one-time code for tokens and stores nothing; the tokens are kept in your iPhone's Keychain. Data imported from WHOOP or Oura stays on your phone.

What the developer collects

Nothing. No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking, no crash reporting.

Deleting your data

Settings → Data & backup → Reset everything deletes all Liftspark data on the phone, signs you out of connected services and forgets paired sensors. Deleting the app does the same. Data already written to Apple Health can be removed in the Health app.
