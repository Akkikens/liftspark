Liftspark

Lift with a plan. See every rep's effort.

Strength training, with your heart rate

  • Plans and guided workouts set by set, with progression that adds weight when you're ready
  • Live heart rate from Apple Watch or any Bluetooth heart-rate strap, with zones and a strain score for every session
  • Calories burned, plus food logging by search or barcode with protein, carbs and fat
  • Workouts, weigh-ins and food go to Apple Health
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Your training stays on your iPhone.
