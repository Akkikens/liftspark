Contact

Email hello@getwingline.com with your iPhone model and iOS version, or open an issue on GitHub. We usually reply within two days.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is stored on your iPhone. iCloud sync is optional and uses your own private iCloud database.

Which heart-rate sensors work?

Apple Watch, and any Bluetooth heart-rate sensor that uses the standard Heart Rate Service, such as most chest straps. Some watches and bands can broadcast heart rate over Bluetooth too.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Settings, Devices and Health, Sync with Apple Health. You choose which data Liftspark can read and write.

How is the strain score worked out?

From the time you spend in each heart-rate zone during a session, scaled from 0 to 21. It's a training guide, not a medical measurement.

Where does food data come from?

Open Food Facts, plus USDA FoodData Central for generic foods. Only your search text or the barcode is sent.

How do I delete my data?

Settings, Data and backup, Reset everything. Data already in Apple Health can be removed in the Health app.

Is Liftspark open source?

Yes, under the GNU AGPL v3.0. The full source is on GitHub.
